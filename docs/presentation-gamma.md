Anaesthetic Allergy Clinic Management System
* A new digital tool for the RPAH Anaesthetic Allergy Clinic
* Department of Clinical Immunology & Allergy
* March 2026
---
The Problem: Why We Need This
* Patient data lives across REDCap exports, spreadsheets, and paper notes — nothing is unified
* SPT and IDT results are handwritten during testing and manually transcribed later, risking errors
* No consolidated view of clinic-wide trends: reaction severity, common agents, or outcomes
* Generating reports and patient handouts requires manual formatting every time
---
Our Solution: One Tool for the Clinic
* A web-based application purpose-built for the anaesthetic allergy testing workflow
* Access patient records, record test results, and generate reports — all in one place
* Works on any device with a browser, including offline on clinic tablets
* Imports directly from REDCap CSV exports — no double handling of data
---
Dashboard & Analytics
* Searchable patient database with pagination and advanced filtering
* At-a-glance statistics: total patients, severe reactions, abandoned procedures, average reaction time
* Filter by reaction grade, date range, hospital, outcome, or suspected agents
* Drill into any patient record to see full clinical history and reaction timeline
---
Skin Testing & Documentation
* Structured recording of SPT, IDT (1:100, 1:10, Neat), and drug challenge outcomes
* Capture reaction timing, observed symptoms, and treatment interventions
* Auto-generated clinical reports and patient handouts — ready to print
* Testing plan generator pre-populated from patient history and suspected agents
---
Data Management & Accessibility
* Upload REDCap CSV exports with built-in validation and duplicate detection
* Offline-capable Progressive Web App — install on clinic laptops and tablets
* Dark mode with automatic device preference detection
* WCAG 2.1 accessible: keyboard navigation, screen reader support, adjustable fonts
